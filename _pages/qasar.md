The QASAR seminar is a bi-weekly seminar on quantum error correction and fault-tolerant quantum computing held on Mondays from 17:00 to 18:00 Paris time.

Subscribe to the mailing list by going to [https://sympa.inria.fr/sympa/subscribe/qasar.seminar](https://sympa.inria.fr/sympa/subscribe/qasar.seminar) or by sending an email to [sympa_inria@inria.fr](mailto:sympa_inria@inria.fr?subject=subscribe%20qasar.seminar%40inria.fr) with subject line “subscribe [qasar.seminar@inria.fr](mailto:qasar.seminar@inria.fr)” (and blank body message).

The seminar archive is available [here](https://mybox.inria.fr/d/c195bec0ef7a40739540/).

A dedicated calendar can be found here: [download the calendar](https://zimbra.inria.fr/home/michael.vasmer@inria.fr/QASAR.ics), [subscribe to the calendar](webcals://zimbra.inria.fr/home/michael.vasmer@inria.fr/QASAR), and below. You can also [open the calendar directly](https://zimbra.inria.fr/home/michael.vasmer@inria.fr/QASAR.html).
