My research focuses on **quantum error correction** and **fault-tolerant quantum computation**: the theory needed to make quantum computers reliable at scale. These problems sit at a rich intersection of computer science, physics, and mathematics, and solving them is a necessary step toward practically useful quantum computation.

I completed my PhD at [University College London](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/mathematical-physical-sciences/quantum) under the supervision of [Dan Browne](https://www.homepages.ucl.ac.uk/~ucapdeb/), then held a joint postdoctoral fellowship at the [Perimeter Institute](https://perimeterinstitute.ca) and the [Institute for Quantum Computing](https://uwaterloo.ca/institute-for-quantum-computing), where my mentors were [Raymond Laflamme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raymond_Laflamme) and [Daniel Gottesman](https://www.cs.umd.edu/~dgottesm/). Before joining INRIA, I was a Senior Quantum Architecture Scientist at [Xanadu Quantum Technologies](https://xanadu.ai) and a Research Scientist at the Perimeter Institute.
